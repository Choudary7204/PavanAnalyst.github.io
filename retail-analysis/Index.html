Key Finding: Loyal buyers (16% of base) drive 41% of revenue. A 5% uplift in retention = +$3.7M additional annual revenue.
Key Insights & Recommendations
01 · Double Down on E-commerce: E-commerce grew +72% YoY, now the #2 channel. Invest in digital shelf optimisation and D2C subscription models.
02 · Target the 35–44 Loyalty Core: Highest loyalty rate (31.8%) and basket size. Develop loyalty tiers with personalised promotions for this cohort.
03 · Reduce 1x Buyer Churn: 34% of buyers only purchased once. Implement post-purchase email sequences and trial-to-loyalty discount offers.
04 · Capitalise on Q4 Momentum: Q4 drives 37% of annual revenue. Plan inventory 60 days early and pre-load digital campaigns from mid-September.
05 · Personal Care Acceleration: Fastest category at +31% growth. Increase SKU depth and allocate 25% more trade spend in FY 2025.
06 · Club Store Volume Play: Club channel grew +24% YoY; multi-pack SKUs drove 68% of club revenue — expand bulk SKU assortment.
